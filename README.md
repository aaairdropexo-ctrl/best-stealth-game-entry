**PASAR MALAM — Silent Stalls**: a complete, original top-down stealth game set in an Indonesian night market. You are Rani, the lantern thief: steal 12 heirloom lanterns across three heists in one night and slip out through the EXIT gate unseen. Eleven named market guards (Pak Darno, Bu Sari, Mas Joko, Mbak Rini, Pak Tono, Bu Ratna, Pak Budi, Mbak Sinta, Kang Dedi, Pak Agus, Mbak Dewi) patrol with amber vision cones, more joining each heist as the market grows watchful. Patrol routes are drawn on the floor with dotted lines, arrows, and a ghost marker showing where each guard will be in 3 seconds. Sound is visible: guard footsteps bloom as expanding rings, and a thrown coin (Q / click) rings out to lure a guard away. The floor itself is the safety map: deep indigo shadow tiles are safe, amber lamplight is exposed, and the douse gadget (E, near a lamp) snuffs a lantern out to make new shadow. Detection builds when you are seen, spikes on a near miss ("CLOSE ONE!"), and a full alarm means a chase ("SPOTTED!") — break line of sight to recover. Every run is scored: +100 a lantern, +25 a near miss, +150 a ghost heist, and a chase costs one strike and −50. Press V on the title screen to watch the patrols replay before you begin.
